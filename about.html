About Salted Soul

A modern snack brand redefining healthy snacking with premium roasted fox nuts.

Our Story

Salted Soul is a modern snack brand dedicated to redefining healthy snacking with premium roasted fox nuts (makhana). The brand was built by a passionate team with a shared vision of transforming traditional fox nuts into flavorful, nutritious, and high-quality snacks.

Our mission is to build a globally recognized snack brand, starting with delicious roasted fox nuts and innovative flavors that offer a healthier alternative to conventional snacks.

At Salted Soul, quality is at the heart of everything we do. We carefully select our ingredients and maintain strict processing standards to deliver snacks that we would proudly serve to our own families.

Looking ahead, our vision goes beyond the domestic market. We aim to expand Salted Soul internationally, bringing the goodness of Indian fox nuts to snack lovers around the world.

Our Mission

To create delicious and healthy snack options that combine traditional ingredients with modern flavors.

Our Vision

To become a globally recognized snack brand bringing the goodness of Indian fox nuts to consumers worldwide.

Our Values

Authenticity, quality ingredients, and a commitment to delivering snacks we would proudly serve to our own families.
