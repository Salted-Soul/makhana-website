What Is Makhana and Its Nutritional Value

Makhana (fox nuts) comes from the Euryale ferox plant and has been used in Indian diets for centuries.

NutrientValue (per 100g)
Protein9–10g
Carbohydrates75–77g
Fat0.5–1g
Fiber7–8g
Calories340–350 kcal

Rich in calcium, magnesium, potassium, and iron.

Why Makhana Is Called a Superfood

  • Supports bones and heart
